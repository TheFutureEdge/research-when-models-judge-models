# Proposed prospective evaluation - not registered

Prepared 2026-10-01, before any archived target matures. This is a proposal for a
separate outcome paper. It is not evidence of historical preregistration and
must be fixed and publicly timestamped before outcomes are examined.

## Frozen methods

Preserve each individual source report, both synthesizers' original blinded
baselines, equal-report step pooling and equal-model step pooling. Archive all
treatments as robustness comparisons, without selecting the best after observing
realized outcomes. Keep completed-path pooling as a separately named operator.

## Candidate primary endpoint

Twelve-month absolute cumulative price-return error, in percentage points,
reported per asset and as an unweighted panel mean. Complement with quarter-level
errors, direction agreement and cross-sectional rank correlation. Treat quarters
and model outputs on the same asset as dependent. No calibration claim is
appropriate without predictive distributions. No portfolio-performance claim
without a separately fixed investable strategy and costs.

## Settlement proposal requiring prospective fixation

For equities, use the last regular-session close on or before the original target
date, preserving the issued date in the data. Use split-consistent prices on the
archived anchor basis, without adding dividends to price-return predictions.
For Bitcoin, settle at 00:00 UTC on the target date using a specified exchange or
composite and an explicit observation-window rule. Choose and freeze the vendor
before scoring; record changes, delistings, missing observations and stale prices.

The first four equity dates are 2026-12-18, 2027-03-18, 2027-06-18 and 2027-09-18.
Bitcoin dates are 2026-12-20, 2027-03-20, 2027-06-20 and 2027-09-20.
The fourth equity target falls on Saturday. Do not silently change issued dates.

## Remaining decisions

Freeze the precise price vendor and calendar, delisting/corporate-action rules,
missing-value policy, primary method contrast, and dependence-aware uncertainty
reporting. Publish the registered plan before looking at outcomes. Expand the
sample only under a prospective rule; report the current seven separately.
